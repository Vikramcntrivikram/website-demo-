With AI-generated illustrations and bilingual storytelling, we aim to make Indian mythology accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

How It Works

1. Ask a Question

Type your question about an Indian god in the chat interface. For example: "Tell me the story of Ganesha" or "What is the significance of Shiva's trident?"

2. Get a Story

Our AI processes your query and generates an engaging story in your selected language (English or Kannada), complete with cultural context and moral lessons.

3. Generate an Image

Click the 'Generate Image' button to create a visual representation of the story or deity you just learned about. Choose your preferred art style.

4. Save and Share

Save your favorite stories and images to your personal gallery, or share them with friends and family via social media.
